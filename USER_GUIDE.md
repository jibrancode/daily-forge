# Daily Forge — User Guide & Manual

Welcome to **Daily Forge** — your private, offline-first daily journaling companion. This guide explains how to get the most out of Daily Forge.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Home Dashboard
- When you launch Daily Forge, the Home Screen greets you with today's date, an encouraging daily reflection quote, your current streak counter, and a one-tap button to **"Forge Today's Entry"**.

### 1.2 "Close My Day" (Journal Screen)
Daily Forge uses a structured daily reflection framework:
1. **Mood Rating**: Rate your overall feeling for the day from 1 (Terrible) to 5 (Great).
2. **Habits Checklist**: Check off daily routines (Workout, Study, Reading, Hydration, Healthy Eating) or add custom habits.
3. **Tasks & Action Items**: Keep track of key items completed today or set for tomorrow.
4. **4 Reflection Prompts**:
   - **Win of the Day**: Highlight what went well.
   - **Key Lesson**: Capture an insight or takeaway.
   - **Improvement**: Identify what could be better tomorrow.
   - **Tomorrow's Priority**: Define your #1 focus.
5. **Freeform Notes**: Write unconstrained thoughts or journal entries.
6. Click **"Close My Day & Save"** to save your reflection locally.

---

## 2. History & Archives

- Switch between **Calendar Grid** and **Timeline Feed** views.
- **Calendar Grid**: View your month at a glance with mood emojis stamped on each date. Click any date to view or add a reflection.
- **Timeline Feed**: Filter reflections using keywords or mood pill buttons (All, Great, Good, Okay, Low, Terrible).

---

## 3. Statistics & Analytics

- View your **Current Streak**, **Best Streak**, **Habit Completion %**, and **Task Completion %**.
- Track your daily mood scores on an interactive **Mood Trend Chart**.
- Inspect your overall mood distribution pie chart and habit success rate comparison.
- Switch time range filters between **7 Days**, **30 Days**, or **All Time**.

---

## 4. Data Export & Backup

Daily Forge is 100% private. Your entries never leave your device. You can export your data anytime:
- **PDF Document**: Formatted PDF ready for printing or digital archiving.
- **Word (.docx)**: Editable Microsoft Word document formatted by headings and dates.
- **Plain Text (.txt)**: Clean text file readable in any text editor.
- **JSON Backup**: Download a complete snapshot file of all entries and settings. You can import this JSON file on any other device to restore your journal.

---

## 5. Themes & Personalization

In **Settings**, you can:
- Switch between **Dark Mode** and **Light Mode**.
- Choose from 6 accent color themes: Emerald, Indigo, Violet, Amber, Rose, Cyan.
- Scale font sizes (Small, Medium, Large).
